Privacy-First Architecture

How DataLens Protects Your Workflow

Designed to minimize data retention with transparent, temporary processing.

No Account Required

No signup, passwords, or emails. We never track your personal identity.

No Dataset Library

Uploaded files are never saved to a permanent database or persistent file catalog.

Random Session IDs

Cryptographically unguessable random session tokens isolate temporary workspaces.

Automatic TTL Expiration

Temporary sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and are automatically purged.

Privacy Note: Uploaded datasets are processed temporarily in isolated server sessions. For confidential or regulated data, review our Privacy Policy before using AI-powered analysis.
